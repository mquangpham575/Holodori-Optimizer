# HOLOLIVE DREAMS tips for DUMMIES

## 1. Important Adjustments for Beginners

| Tip | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Adjust Note Speed** | Avoid playing at very low speeds (e.g., 2). Recommended speed is between **8 and 10.9** for better timing and reaction. |
| **Use Offset Timing** | Settings include offset timing to adjust when notes register (early or late), helping with timing issues. |
| **Look at the Top of the Screen** | Beginners tend to look at their fingers or bottom of screen, but you should focus at the top where notes appear earlier for better reaction time. |

## 2. Flick Notes Technique
- Despite the arrow pointing up on flick notes, **flicking left or right** is often better.
- This reduces the time needed to reposition your finger for the next note.
- Crucial for playing hard or expert songs efficiently.

## 3. Progression & Difficulty

| Strategy | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Gradually Increase Difficulty** | Don't stick to songs you can easily play; slowly push to higher difficulty even if you perform poorly at first. |
| **Use Multiple Fingers** | For songs around levels 26-29, using more than two fingers is recommended for better control. |
| **Avoid PC for Beginners** | Playing on PC is much harder unless experienced with similar six-lane keyboard games (e.g., Osu!mania). |

## 4. Playstyle Tips
- **Pattern Recognition**: Learning common note patterns helps in achieving full combos on high-level songs. Patterns often repeat across different songs.
- **Avoid Excessive Force**: Do **not** smash or mash your screen harder thinking it improves performance. Light taps are sufficient; pressing too hard can cause fatigue or injury, such as carpal tunnel syndrome.
- **Visual Distractions**: Some games, like Holodori, add flashy effects and character animations that can be distracting.
- **Comfortable Setup**: Maintain a comfortable posture to avoid strain or injury during gameplay.

## 5. Improving Performance in Holodori

### Ranking and Score System
- **Ranking is not solely based on perfect play**: Character unit and leader performance heavily influence scores.
- You must play all songs associated with your character to improve overall rating.

### Character Management for Higher Scores

| Aspect | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Leveling Characters** | Increase character levels to boost stats. |
| **SP Training** | Raises character card levels further. |
| **Blooming** | Pulling duplicate cards grants bonuses, called "blooming." |
| **Character Skills** | Each card has specific skills (special, active, passive) that affect scoring support and stats. |

### Example: Marine's Skills

| Skill Type | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Outfit Skill** | With 2 or more Gen 3 Members, grants All Stats UP 50% to all. |
| **Special Skill** | For 12s, Grants Score Support Effect of 120%. Skill Activation Rate UP 40%. |
| **Active Skill** | For 10s, Score UP 115%. Every 23s with a High Probability chance. |
| **Passive Skill** | With 2 or more Gen 3 Members, grants Score Support Effect of 12% to 2 Gen 3 Members. |

### Team Synergy and Leader Choice
- Team synergy depends on matching character types and skills.
- Example: Marine's skills focus on Gen 3 members. If your team lacks Gen 3 members, her skills are less effective.
- Choose a **leader character** whose outfit skills benefit your whole team.

| Leader Character | Outfit Skill Effect (from Database) | Team Compatibility Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Houshou Marine** | With 2 or more Gen 3 Members, grants All Stats UP 50% to all. | Requires at least two Gen 3 members for max effect. |
| **Moona Hoshinova** | With 2 or more ID Gen 1 Members, grants All Stats UP 50% to all. | Requires at least two ID Gen 1 members for max effect. |

### Member and Skill Boards
- **Holo Member Board** provides multiple buffs:
  - **Red**: Leader skills
  - **Yellow**: Song bonuses
  - **Green**: Overall boosts
  - **Blue**: Member skills (highly recommended to max for better stats)
- Resetting boards is possible by spending gold to get materials back.

### Song Bonuses
- Some songs give bonuses if played by the character associated with the song.
- Having the corresponding leader on that song increases score further.

### Memory Board
- Progressing through the story unlocks memories that give percentage boosts to unit stats.
- Can be swapped for different bonuses.
- Provides a small but helpful boost (e.g., 4.2% unit stats increase).
